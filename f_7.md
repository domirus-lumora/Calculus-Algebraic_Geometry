## Combination

y is gonna meet with 2 doctors, and there is 4 doctors.  We could ignore the sequence because meet with intern A + intern B is same as meet with intern B + intern A.

So, this question will be challenging.

But it is same as choose 2 doctors from 4 doctors, lets let the n become 4, and m become 2. C(n,m), the **Combinaterial Number**.

$$C(n,m) = \frac{n!}{m!(n-m)!} $$

You might think they are angry or rage, actually the `!` symbol is means factorial. For example, $3!=1·2·3=6$， $7!=1·2·3·4·5·6·7=5040$.

Now, let's put the parameter:
$$C(4,2) = \frac{4!}{2!(4-2)!} $$
$$= \frac{1·2·3·4}{1·2(4-2)!} $$
$$= \frac{24}{2(2)!} $$
$$= \frac{24}{2(1·2)} $$
$$= \frac{24}{2(2)} $$
$$= \frac{24}{4} $$
$$= 6 $$

So there is 6 combination,let's list out and verify:

Intern A = IA, Intern B = IB, Expert A = EA, Expert B = EB.

$$IA,IB;EA,EB;IA,EA;IB,EB;IB,EA;IA,EB;$$

There is 6.

---

If we cared about sequeance:

$$IA,IB;IB,IA;EA,EB;EB,EA;IA,EA;IB,EB;EB,IB;
IB,EA;IA,EB;EB,IA;$$

There is 12.

How we calculate it? By using Permutation.

$$P(n,m) = \frac{n!}{(n-m)!}$$
$$= \frac{4!}{(4-2)!}$$
$$= \frac{4!}{2!}$$
$$= \frac{24}{2}$$
$$=12$$

---
> "These body check have 5 choice, but y can only pick 3, and the sequence of each check is not important."

$$C(5,3)=\frac{5!}{3!(5-3)!}$$
$$=\frac{120}{6(2)!}$$
$$=\frac{120}{6(2)}$$
$$=\frac{120}{12}$$
$$=10$$

---

Lastly, multiply all of the possibility:
$$3·2·6·10=360$$

We use multiply because all choice are independent and must follow the process. We only use add while there is either one.
