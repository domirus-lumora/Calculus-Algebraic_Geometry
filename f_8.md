# Statistics

Let's calculate data~

There is two groups of datas. A = {10,10,10,10,10}, B = {0,10,20,10}. The all data in the group A are same, which also means no difference. Unlike group B, the datas have difference although that difference is not big. But if group B is your income in a week, then you might think there is a huge difference.

## Average

Do you all know about average?

$$\frac{(Datas's Sum)}{The Quantity Of Datas}$$

Let's calculate group A's average number:
$$\frac{(10+10+10+10+10)}{5} $$
$$=\frac{50}{5} $$
$$=10 $$

So its average is 10. We don't surprise about it because if the datas are same, then the average will be same.

---
Now, let's calculate group B's average.

$$\frac{(0+10+20+10)}{4} $$
$$=\frac{40}{4} $$
$$=10 $$

Although there is different data, but its average also 10. If these two groups of datas's average is the same, can we said that these two group are same? No, although their average is same, but its data is not same, so we can't said that these two datas are same.

We also called average as mean.

## Deviation

After calculating the average, we could calculate the deviation of two groups. Deviation is how far is the data to the average point, or we called as, the center point. Mathematically:

$$x-\mu = y$$

If the y is a positive integer, that is mean the deviation is positive, vice versa. And if the y is 0, that is mean that data is same as the average.

Let's calculate the deviation of group B.
$$0-10=-10$$
$$10-10=0$$
$$20-10=10$$
$$10-10=0$$
$$(-10)+10+0+0=0$$

Did you realize that the sum of deviation is equal to 0?

## How to let negative disappear?

We could use || (absolute value) to let the negative disappear, but absolute value's function can't been derived or been differentiate.

We could use the square of the number to let the negative disappear. This is because while the same simbol multiplied together, they will become positive.

There might has other way to let the negative symbol disappear, but the square is the easiest and useful way.

So, if the deviation is squared, we called it as squared deviation.

## Variance

Now, let's add all of the squared deviation.

$$(-10)^2 = 100$$
$$0^2=0$$
$$10^2=100$$
$$0^2-0$$

$$100+0+100+0=100+100=200$$

And divided it by the quantity of datas.

$$\frac{200}{4}=50$$

So, this 50 is the variance of group B.

## Standard deviation

If we square root the variance:
$$\sqrt{50} \approx 7.07$$

So, 7.07 is the standard deviation of group B. This standard diviation means, almost every data is within 7.07 of the mean. Which means, when me add 7.07 to the average:

$$10+7.07=17.07$$
It is close to 20, isn't it?

But this standard deviation need many datas, so it will be more accurate to the real possibility.

Why it will be more accurate?

## Law of large number

The name of this law is funny, but it is intuitive. This law said that when the sample are huge is quantity, the standard deviation will more accurate, and the possibility will apporoach to the real possibility.

This is because with more data, the influence of any single extreme value becomes smaller, so the average gets closer to the truth.
